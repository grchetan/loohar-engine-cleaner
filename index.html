Who We Are

Built For The Indian Garage

Lohar Auto Garage is an Indian startup focused on manufacturing and supplying high-quality automotive cleaning solutions. Our products are designed to remove grease, oil, dirt and stubborn engine deposits efficiently while being easy to use for garages and vehicle owners.

We understand the challenges faced by Indian mechanics and garage owners. That's why every product we make is specifically formulated for the Indian market — powerful enough for heavy-duty commercial use, yet safe and easy for everyday vehicle owners.
